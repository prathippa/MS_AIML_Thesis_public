# Prospective extension protocol, frozen 4 November 2026

The authoritative machine-readable specification is prospective_plan.json. It was fixed before new evaluation model calls. This is a local timestamped protocol, not third-party preregistration.

Primary: GPT-5 recommendation per product; combined agreement/schedule/confidence priority versus confidence only at 50% coverage. Outcome: exact-code selective risk. Paired product-bootstrap 95% interval (2,000 draws, seed 7) entirely below zero indicates support. All other comparisons are secondary/descriptive. Original thesis hypotheses and their verdicts remain unchanged.

Cohort: 200 development and 400 evaluation products from the pinned ATLAS training file, after the documented duplicate audit. Development predictions calibrate confidence and support technical diagnostics only. The fixed policies are not selected or changed on these outcomes. The 400-case size targets roughly five-percentage-point interval half-width under an extrapolation from the old ATLAS paired analysis; no formal power guarantee is claimed.

Both generators use a 4,000 output-token cap, a documented difference from the original 8,000-token experiments. Truncations/malformed outputs remain failures. No silent model substitutions, test-set resplitting or after-the-fact exclusion of historical gold codes. Failed designated outputs are referred. A maximum of one malformed-output retry follows the preserved generation implementation; API errors are retained without automatic retries.

Budget: EUR100 total maximum; every attempt permanently reserves EUR0.075 against a shared ledger. The planned 1,200 calls reserve EUR90; the request cap of 1,320 reserves at most EUR99. Bounds include output reasoning tokens and a conservative currency/fee contingency. Monetary reservations are upper bounds, not actual billed charges. Keys stay in the external credential file and are never included in the thesis package.

Limits: the source is a public benchmark training split, not a contamination-free dataset; ruling IDs are absent; deduplication is heuristic; historical golds do not all appear in the audited tariff release. The follow-up is independent of the earlier study's generated outputs, but these limits still constrain generalisation.
